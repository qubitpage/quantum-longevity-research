Abstract Rezumat

We present a systematic quantum-chemical study of ten compounds with established or emerging evidence for human lifespan extension, computed using Complete Active Space Configuration Interaction (CASCI) with GPU-accelerated exact diagonalization and Variational Quantum Eigensolver (VQE) validation on an AMD MI300X accelerator. All ten molecules achieved chemical accuracy (< 1 kcal/mol) with an average VQE error of 0.249 kcal/mol across 8 independent validation critics. Complementary DFT B3LYP calculations provided HOMO-LUMO gaps, dipole moments, ionization potentials, and electrophilicity indices. We identify three mechanistically distinct clusters and propose an evidence-based natural formulation with specific plant sources and dosing.

1. Introduction 1. Introducere

1.1 Why Quantum Chemistry for Longevity? 1.1 De ce chimie cuantică pentru longevitate?

Aging is driven by twelve hallmarks — from genomic instability to cellular senescence. A growing number of compounds can modulate these hallmarks, but their electronic structure — which determines binding affinity, redox chemistry, and radical scavenging — has not been systematically characterized with quantum methods. Classical force fields cannot capture the multi-reference electron correlation governing these interactions.

This study uses CASCI (Complete Active Space CI) — a method that explicitly treats electron-electron interaction in a selected orbital window — to characterize how complex each molecule's electronic structure truly is. This complexity correlates with the versatility of biological interactions.

Îmbătrânirea este determinată de douăsprezece repere — de la instabilitatea genomică la senescența celulară. Un număr crescând de compuși pot modula aceste repere, dar structura lor electronică nu a fost caracterizată sistematic cu metode cuantice. Câmpurile de forță clasice nu pot capta corelația electronică multi-referință. Acest studiu folosește CASCI pentru a caracteriza complexitatea structurii electronice — o complexitate care se corelează cu versatilitatea interacțiunilor biologice.

1.2 Objectives 1.2 Obiective

  1. Compute ground-state electronic structure of 10 longevity compounds using CASCI
  2. Validate with VQE at chemical accuracy (< 1 kcal/mol)
  3. Characterize reactivity via DFT descriptors (HOMO-LUMO, dipole, hardness, electrophilicity)
  4. Rank compounds by electron correlation energy
  5. Map to natural plant sources with practical dosing

4.1 Key Insights 4.1 Concluzii cheie

Urolithin A stands out with the highest dipole moment (10.7 D) — more than double any other compound. This extreme polarity drives its selective accumulation in mitochondria, where the membrane potential gradient favors polar molecules. This is consistent with its observed role as a mitophagy activator.

The Dasatinib-Quercetin synergy explained: Dasatinib (low IP: 6.24 eV) primarily disrupts kinase signaling, while Quercetin (highest IP: 8.50 eV) directly destabilizes BCL-2 anti-apoptotic complexes. They attack senescent cells from two different electronic angles — complementary, not redundant.

Fisetin is the most reactive molecule (HOMO-LUMO gap: 0.36 eV, electrophilicity: 132 eV) — consistent with its dual role as radical scavenger and senolytic agent.

Urolitina A iese în evidență cu cel mai mare moment dipolar (10,7 D) — mai mult decât dublu față de orice alt compus. Această polaritate extremă conduce la acumularea selectivă în mitocondrii. Sinergia Dasatinib-Quercetină: atacă celulele senescente din două unghiuri electronice diferite — complementare, nu redundante. Fisetina este cea mai reactivă moleculă — consistentă cu rolul ei dublu de captator de radicali și agent senolitic.

6. Limitations & Improvements 6. Limitări și îmbunătățiri

Current limitations:

  1. Minimal basis set (STO-3G): Absolute energies are ~5-15% from the complete basis set limit. Relative rankings are reliable.
  2. Small active space CAS(4,4): Only 4 of 50-74 total electrons explicitly correlated. Frontier orbital region only.
  3. Gas-phase only: No solvent effects (water, membrane). Real biological environments would alter electronic structure.
  4. Fragment approximation: Rapamycin (MW=914) and dasatinib (MW=488) modeled as pharmacophore fragments.
  5. No protein-ligand interaction: We characterized isolated molecules, not drug-target complexes.
  6. Static geometries: No geometry optimization at CASCI level.
  7. No zero-point energy: Vibrational corrections (~1-5 kcal/mol) not included.
Limitări actuale: 1. Set de bază minimal (STO-3G) — energiile absolute sunt ~5-15% de la limita setului complet de bază. 2. Spațiu activ mic CAS(4,4) — doar 4 din 50-74 electroni total. 3. Doar fază gazoasă — fără efecte de solvent. 4. Aproximare prin fragment pentru rapamicină și dasatinib. 5. Fără interacțiune proteină-ligand. 6. Geometrii statice. 7. Fără energie de punct zero.

7. Conclusions 7. Concluzii

  1. All ten longevity compounds achieved chemical accuracy (< 1 kcal/mol VQE error) using GPU-accelerated CASCI on a single AMD MI300X in under 3 minutes.
  2. Compounds cluster into three anti-aging strategies: cellular cleanup (urolithin A, spermidine, rapamycin), senolytic (dasatinib, quercetin, fisetin), and metabolic repair (NMN, metformin, resveratrol, AKG).
  3. Urolithin A emerged as the most electronically complex compound (highest correlation: -887 kcal/mol, highest dipole: 10.7 D), consistent with its broad-spectrum mitophagy activity.
  4. A practical plant-based formulation was derived, centered on pomegranate, wheat germ, strawberries, capers/onions, barberry root, and Japanese knotweed, with intermittent fasting as the natural rapamycin analog.
  5. Key limitation: Food-derived doses are 10-100× below clinical trial doses, except wheat germ spermidine and pomegranate ellagitannins which approach therapeutic ranges.
